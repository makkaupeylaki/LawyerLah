About lawyerlah

You describe an everyday situation in Singapore, and lawyerlah points to which Act and section seems to be relevant, then explains what that section says in plain English and what is unclear or depends on the facts.

For now it only covers a few sections on three topics: tenancy, employment and traffic. If nothing fits, it says the question is not covered.

It never tells you whether something is allowed or not, never gives a verdict and never tells you what to do. Each answer links to the section on Singapore Statutes Online so you can read the actual wording, and lists official routes that may apply (such as MOM, HDB, the Small Claims Tribunals, Pro Bono SG or the police) with their conditions and links.

While the law text is still being prepared, answers built on placeholder text are marked DEMO DATA, not real law and have no link to a section.

You can ask up to 10 questions a day. Your IP address is not stored: only a one-way hash of it is used to count questions, and it expires after two days. If you mark an answer as Helpful or Wrong, the question and the section shown are saved so the answers can be improved.
